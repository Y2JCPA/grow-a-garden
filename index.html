❓ How to Play

  1. 🌱Buy seeds at the Seed Shop — new stock every 5 minutes!
  2. 🏡Go to your garden and plant anywhere on the dirt
  3. 🧺Harvest fruit when it sparkles. Trees and bushes keep growing more!
  4. 💰Sell your fruit to Steven. Heavier fruit is worth more
  5. 🥚Buy pet eggs, hatch them in your garden — pets help you grow

✨ Gold (20x) and 🌈 Rainbow (50x) fruit are super rare!

🌧️ Rain → 💧 Wet (2x) • ❄️ Frost → Chilled, or 🧊 Frozen (10x) if Wet • ⛈️ Lightning → ⚡ Shocked (100x)

🌙 Night → Moonlit • 🩸 Blood Moon → Bloodlit • 🌠 Meteors → Celestial (120x)

💦 Sprinklers make nearby plants grow faster and mutate more. 🚿 Watering cans speed up one plant.

⭐ Favorite a fruit in your backpack to keep it when you sell.

🌍 Play together: on the start screen tap Play together with family — everyone types the same secret code and you share one world, each with your own garden!
