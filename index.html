❓ How to Play

  1. 🌱Walk to a dirt patch and plant a seed
  2. 💧Water it — wet plants grow faster! Refill at the well
  3. 🧺When it sparkles, harvest it
  4. 💰Sell crops to Shlomo for coins
  5. 🛒Buy better seeds, upgrades and new garden plots

✨ Golden and 🌈 Rainbow plants are super rare and worth a LOT!

🌧️ Crops harvested after rain are 💧 Wet (1.5x). At night you might get 🌙 Moonlit crops (2x)!

♻️ Some plants grow back after you harvest them.

🐕 Stay near Buddy — he digs up treasure!
